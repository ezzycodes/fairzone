SOCIAL RESPONSIBILITY

Given the history of South Africa’s socio-economic dynamics social responsibility should be the commitment and privilege of all companies. As mentioned in the1994 inauguration speech; “Our daily deeds as ordinary South Africans must produce an actual South African reality that will reinforce humanity`s belief in justice, strengthen its confidence in the nobility of the human soul and sustain all our hopes for a glorious life for all”. In playing our part to create a better South Africa, Fair Zone Trade cc has made a commitment to consistently provide means and accomplish the following:
  • Unemployment and economic inactivity are serious challenges in South Africa; our aim is not only to create employment but to create entry level positions. These positions are mainly aimed at school-leavers who have no means to further their studies, no skills to base a career on and no experience. While South African corporate strategies have focused on graduate programs and learnerships a large segment of youth that fall outside the requirement brackets still struggle to gain meaningful employment.
  • In addition to opening up entry level positions we aim to create growth opportunities through our Training and Skills Development program. We offer our employees in house training as well opportunities to gain industry related qualifications and certification.
  • We support SME’s and local business through outsourcing skills and services for our internal IT, Financial and admin related services. We believe in a fair distribution of wealth by outsourcing SME’s who specialize in the services they offer as a result driving broad based economic growth for all.
 

QUALITY ASSURENCE PROCESS

We take a great deal of pride in the quality of the product and services we deliver and as a precaution against poor service delivery we always implement the following simple steps in our quality assurance process. We use only the best and well serviced tools and machines. Every trade has its tools and it is a well-known fact that the better the tools the more polished the product. We make it a point to keep up with the technological advancements in our field and use the best tools to deliver quality workmanship and therefore also assuring safety. Quality components and materials are also used in creating our products. We purchase our raw material from reputable resellers. We refuse to compromise on the quality of materials to boost bottom-line revenues We consider perfection as a well implemented plan. We plan every project down to the last detail, making sure the every phase is completed with seamless perfection and accuracy. All our staff members work under close super vision and we pay attention to detail in the supervision process so that all possible errors are vetted and rectified with as early as possible. Our final inspection process happens at the completion of the project, we ensure that everything is well installed properly and is in good working order. In the unlikely event that there are flaws we correct them prior to the official completion of the project.

GROWTH

Since its establishment Fair Zone Trade cc has grown by leaps and bounds. At grass roots levels the founder of Fair Zone trade cc was a sole proprietor and practiced perfectionist when he started a small un-funded company in 2007. Nearly a decade later all the financial aspects of Fair Zone have changed and the company is now a recognized SME. Fair Zone has also broadened the service offerings to construction and engineering. We have extended our capacity from domestic to industrial and commercial offerings. We have completed projects in both the private and public sector and have maintained our reputation of delivering quality products.
