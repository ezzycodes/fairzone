Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2025

We respect the privacy of our visitors and are committed to protecting your personal information whenever you visit or communicate with our site. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have regarding your information. We may update this policy from time to time — please review it periodically.

1. Collected Information

We collect and process information during the normal operation of our website in a number of ways, including but not limited to:

  1. Visit and usage data: When you visit our site we may collect technical and usage information such as your IP address (or an anonymized version), approximate location, browser and device details, referral source, pages viewed, timestamps, and other server log data.
  2. Form data: Any information you submit through forms on our website (for example contact or quote request forms) — such as your name, email address, telephone number, company and message — will be collected and used to respond to your enquiry or provide requested services.
  3. Communications: If you communicate with our staff (by email, phone, or via the website), we may retain a record of that correspondence including any personal data you provide during such communications.

2. Cookie Usage

We may use cookies and similar technologies to collect non-identifying, statistical information about site usage. Cookies help us understand how visitors use the site and enable us to improve functionality and user experience.

Cookies do not typically contain personal identifying information. You can control or block cookies through your browser settings, but disabling cookies may reduce the functionality of parts of the site.

Note: third-party advertisers or services may also set cookies when you click on external links or ads on our site. We do not control those cookies and recommend reviewing the privacy policies of the third parties involved.

3. Our Use of Your Information

We use collected information to:

  1. Respond to your requests and enquiries and provide the services or information you request.
  2. Fulfil contractual commitments and service obligations (for example processing an order or providing a quote).
  3. Notify you of updates, improvements or changes to our website, services, or terms — where appropriate.
  4. Contact existing customers about products or services related to prior purchases where you have consented to such contact.
  5. Send marketing communications only when you have given consent. You may withdraw consent at any time (see the Opt Out section).
  6. Provide aggregated, non-identifying data (statistical information) to third parties for analysis or advertising purposes. Such data does not identify you personally.

4. Personal Data Storage

Storage and transfer: We may store or process your information outside the European Economic Area (EEA) where our systems, suppliers or service partners operate. By providing your data, you agree to its storage and processing in those locations.

Security: We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data and keep it on secure servers. Transaction data (where applicable) is encrypted. However, please be aware that no method of online transmission is 100% secure; data sent to us is transmitted at your own risk.

Passwords: If you are provided with access that requires a password, you are responsible for keeping that password confidential and for any activity that occurs under your account.

5. Opt Out & Data Access

Opt-out: If you wish to opt out of marketing communications or withdraw consent to processing for marketing purposes, please contact us (details below) and we will promptly update our records.

Access to your data: If you’d like to request a copy of the personal data we hold about you, please contact us. In some cases we may charge a small administration fee to cover the cost of providing the data.

6. Information Disclosure

We may disclose personal information in the following circumstances:

  1. To members of our corporate group (including subsidiaries or holding companies) where required for business operations.
  2. To a third party buyer or prospective buyer if we sell, merge or transfer any part of our business or assets.
  3. Where required by law, court order or regulatory obligation, or to prevent or investigate fraud or other wrongdoing.

7. Third Party Links

Our site may contain links to third-party websites. These websites have their own privacy practices and policies, which may differ from ours. We are not responsible for the content or practices of those third-party sites and recommend you review their privacy policies before providing personal data.

8. Information Accessibility

If you wish to request information about the personal data we hold about you, or request corrections, please contact us. We will aim to respond to all legitimate requests within a reasonable timeframe. A small administration fee may be charged to cover the cost of processing certain requests.

9. Contacting Us

If you have questions, requests or comments about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please get in touch:

  • Contact page: Visit our contact page
